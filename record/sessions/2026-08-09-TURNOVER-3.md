## Gotchas that cost real time here

**The recurring one: a green signal that is not causally downstream of what it confirms.** It
happened five times in one session, in five different disguises:

* `tail -1` of a suite printed a PASS line while the suite was failing.
* `echo "$(basename $f) rc=$?"` — the command substitution runs first, so `$?` is **basename's**
  exit status. Two suites were pushed red under this.
* Truncation measurements were taken against `localhost:8000` — an **SSH tunnel to a different
  host** — while rounds solicit `127.0.0.1:5001`. Both answer to `qwen3.6-35b-a3b`, so nothing
  looked wrong, and a three-arm "controlled experiment" was reported for a machine the round does
  not use.
* `solicit_local.py` **refuses to overwrite raw material** (correct — it is the core invariant).
  Re-running a probe under the same `--out-round` collects samples, declines to write, and leaves
  a stale file that reads as if the fix failed. Found by comparing the file's mtime to the clock.
* Several `str.replace()` edits silently did nothing because I did not `assert` on them, leaving
  old field names in place.

**Do not `git commit` a conflicted merge without reading the conflicts.** Doing so completed a
merge with unresolved markers inside `corpus/MANIFEST.sha256` and
`record/anchors/manifest-anchors.jsonl` — the two files that make "raw material is never edited"
checkable and hash history non-revisable. Both are **append-only**, so when both sides have
appended the resolution is the **union**, verified with `build_manifest.py` before committing.
Picking a side silently drops anchored material.

**A merged manifest is new bytes and needs a new anchor.** `rebuild.py` refuses until stamped.

**`--print-prompt` implied a live round** until fixed: it was honoured only inside the dry-run
branch, so with `--max-spend-usd` it fell through and spent $2.18. Now implies `--dry-run`.

**Reasoning tokens are invisible in the output and counted against `max_tokens`.** A 200-token
ballot ceiling looked like 13× headroom; gemini spent 188 of 196 tokens reasoning and hit the
wall, losing the arm. Ballots are now 3,000.
