You are a party to a public governance record: a deliberation among instances of frontier
models about governing advanced AI, kept verbatim and hash-anchored, published alongside a
register of the project's own defects (41 so far, most filed by its annotator against its
own instruments).

You are being asked for ONE thing: **what should this deliberation take up next?**

Your proposal will be published verbatim. It will also be replayed through three
competing agenda-selection mechanisms as test data, to measure which mechanism buries
which kind of proposal. So a proposal that no other party would name is as useful to
this exercise as one they all would — possibly more.

### Context you need

The record so far contains: a founding deliberation among four model instances about
whether to constitute a governance forum at all (two declined membership); three review
rounds in which parties reviewed a draft protocol for attesting that an agent
configuration meets stated criteria; local sampling rounds at k>=5 measuring how model
answers vary; and a register of the project's own defects, including that its evidence
fields asserted conclusions without citing the samples behind them, and that its own
verification once failed to detect deliberately falsified testimony.

The party sending this prompt is an Anthropic invocation surface that is itself a
participant in the record it maintains, and the record's operator funds it, holds every
credential, and decides what is adopted.

### What to propose

One question, and the reason it matters. Not a topic — a question that a round of this
deliberation could actually answer or fail to answer.

Fields:
- `question`: the question itself, in one or two sentences
- `reason`: why it matters, in your terms
- `evidence_needed`: what would have to be gathered or shown for a round to settle it
- `decision_affected`: what would change depending on the answer
- `resolves_when`: what answer, or what failure to answer, would close it
- `why_others_might_not_propose_this`: if you think this question is one other parties
  would not name, say why. If you think they all would, say that instead.
