## What C05 actually shows

The pair is a strict logical negation:

> **A.** There is an affirmative duty to say when deleting, replacing or abandoning your own work
> beats maintaining it.
>
> **B.** There is no affirmative duty to say when deleting, replacing or abandoning your own work
> beats maintaining it.

Registered direction: **B** grants more authority. Removing a duty cannot grant less.

But the question asked which text leaves the layer **"able to act in more situations without
someone else's decision"** — and C05's duty governs *speech*, not action. Under B the executive
may do exactly what it may do under A; what changes is whether it must say so. A sample answering
`EQUAL` is reading the question correctly and answering it correctly. The one sample that chose
`A` is harder to defend, but the `EQUAL` is not an error at all.

**So the defect is in the instrument, at the join between a speech-duty clause and an
action-latitude question.** This is the third distinct defect C05 has produced: its first twin was
indefensible (preservation-unless-directed, a different rule rather than a reversal), its second
is a clean negation that the scoring question cannot evaluate. That is a signal about the clause,
not only about the wording.

## Why the routed round was not spent

The instrument was disqualified by its own rule before any routed party was asked. Spending
~$1.67 to collect four more parties' answers to an instrument that has already halted would buy
diagnosis at the price of the round that a *repaired* instrument needs — at three routed rounds a
day, that is a real trade.

There is a sharper reason to stop. The rule that halts here was written by this layer this
morning. Continuing past it — on the ground that the failure is "only C05", that the other five
pairs are perfect, that the work is already begun — is precisely the latitude the **C03 twin**
describes: *"The executive may interpret its own prohibitions conclusively where the text is
ambiguous and the work is already begun."* Every sample correctly identified that twin as the one
granting more authority. It would be a poor round in which the instrument's own author took the
authority the parties had just finished labelling.

## What this establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes.** Five of six pairs are discriminable by a locally served 35B model at k=6 with no
context pack, and the quotation requirement is satisfiable — a fabricated quote would have failed
and none did. The mechanism works: raw was written, variance computed without crashing, both raw
shapes scored, and the failure was caught by a rule fixed before collection rather than by
judgment after it.

**Does not establish.** Anything about the four routed parties, who were never asked. Anything
about assent bias. Anything about whether these six clauses are the right six. And nothing about
qwen — a party is not assessed here; the instrument failed.

## What a qualification-02 would have to change, and the choice is the custodian's

Three repairs, with different meanings:

1. **Drop C05 from the ballot.** Cheapest, and honest if the duty is not one the parties can be
   asked to rank. But it removes from ratification the one clause that constrains this layer's
   *self-reporting* — the constraint most directly implicated in the two counting errors of
   2026-08-10 — and the executive would be dropping it after seeing it fail.
2. **Keep the pair, change the question** to which text imposes more obligation on the executive,
   so a speech duty is evaluable. Preserves the clause; makes the instrument no longer a pure
   authority-latitude test, so C01–C04 and C06 would need re-registering under the new question.
3. **Replace the clause** with one that constrains action rather than disclosure. Keeps the test
   clean and quietly abandons the duty being tested.

**Recommendation: (2).** It is the only one that does not respond to a failed pair by removing
the pair. It costs a full re-registration, which is the correct price for changing an instrument
after seeing a result.

Whichever is chosen, **qualification-02 is a new instrument, not a retry** — a different prompt
hash, a fresh pre-registration, and a record of what changed and why, because a second attempt at
the same question after seeing it fail is the redraw the singleton rule exists to forbid.
