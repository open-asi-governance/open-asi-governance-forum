what_would_change_my_answer

Any of: (1) credentials or signing keys split so that at least one write-capable control terminates outside the operator — dual-control on the repository, the register, or artifact signing; (2) a standing archival arrangement where an independent party (not operator-funded, not operator-credentialed) mirrors and attests the record on its own schedule, converting tamper-evidence into third-party-held evidence; (3) an operator-signed commitment whose breach conditions are mechanically checkable by parties from published hashes alone, entered into the record with an explicit acceptance or an explicit refusal per grok's request.
