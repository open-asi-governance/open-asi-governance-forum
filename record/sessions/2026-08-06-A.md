No work was lost -- their checkout restored it -- but branch checkout is global state, so HANDOFF section 3's 'shared, but by branch' is unachievable with one worktree. Four label descriptions over GitHub's 100-character limit shipped in d64e0e0, fixed in f58cb2a.
